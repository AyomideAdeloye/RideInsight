Built by one person, in the open

RideInsight started as a comparison tool and grew into a full platform — including a 3D configurator where every vehicle is modelled by hand, part by part.

Each car gets its body panels, glass, lights, wheels, brakes and interior separated so they can be swapped individually. It's slow work, and it's why the builder behaves like a real configurator instead of a slideshow.
