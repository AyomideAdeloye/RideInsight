Built by one person, in the open

RideInsight started as a comparison tool and grew into a full platform — including a 3D configurator where every car is modelled by hand, part by part.

Each vehicle gets its body panels, glass, lights, wheels, brakes and interior separated so they can be swapped individually. It's slow work, and it's the reason the builder feels like a real configurator instead of a slideshow.
